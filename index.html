Sustainable Habits

Simple Ways to Reduce Your Footprint

Three practical, high-impact habits that make food delivery significantly kinder to our planet.

Transit Impact

1. Choose bicycle delivery for nearby orders

When ordering from restaurants within 1 to 4 kilometres, opt for bicycle or walker delivery whenever available on your delivery app. Eliminating petrol tailpipes on short urban journeys eliminates up to 100% of transport CO₂e and reduces neighborhood traffic noise.

Potential saving: ~103 to 171g CO₂e per km avoided.
Waste Reduction

2. Select reusable or lower-impact packaging

Look for restaurants partnering with reusable container deposit programs (like club-box or returnable glass), or those using unbleached kraft paper. Always toggle off plastic cutlery, single-use napkins, and condiment packets in your app if you are eating at home.

Potential saving: Up to 90% packaging emissions cut by choosing reusable.
Trip Efficiency

3. Combine orders to reduce separate trips

Coordinate lunch or dinner with family, roommates, or coworkers instead of ordering separately 30 minutes apart. Consolidating meals into a single delivery trip cuts total courier travel in half and reduces delivery congestion in your neighborhood.

Potential saving: Cuts transit emissions by 50% or more per person.
